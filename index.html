Who we are

A practice built on documentation, not shortcuts

KVKSN & Co. is a Chartered Accountancy firm at Kadapa serving individuals, MSMEs, partnership firms, companies, LLPs and trusts across Andhra Pradesh. Our core strengths are income tax return filing and GST filing, scrutiny assessments and appeal matters before NFAC and CIT(A), ROC registration and company filings, Udyam and MSME registration, and maintenance of books of account in Tally.

We are also empanelled concurrent auditors with Indian Bank, Canara Bank and Andhra Pradesh Grameena Bank (APGB), and prepare project reports for bank loan applications across dairy, poultry, manufacturing and MSME sectors.

What we do

Services

Comprehensive tax, compliance and accounting support — tailored to individuals, MSMEs, firms, companies, LLPs and trusts across Rayalaseema.

01

Income Tax Return Filing

ITR-1 to ITR-7 for salaried individuals, businesses, firms, companies and trusts — prepared, verified and filed.

  • Capital gains & house property
  • Presumptive taxation 44AD / 44ADA
  • Refund follow-up & 143(1) matters
02

GST Registration & Return Filing

New registrations, monthly and quarterly returns, annual return and reconciliation.

  • GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, CMP-08
  • GSTR-9 & 9C annual return
  • ITC reconciliation with GSTR-2B
03

Scrutiny & Assessment Cases

End-to-end handling of income tax scrutiny and reassessment proceedings, and GST show-cause notices.

  • Sec 143(2) scrutiny submissions
  • Sec 148 / 148A reassessment replies
  • GST DRC-01 & DRC-03 matters
04

Appeal Cases

Drafting grounds of appeal and statements of facts, with representation before the appellate authorities.

  • CIT(A) / NFAC appeals — Form 35
  • Penalty appeals u/s 270A, 271AAC
  • Stay & rectification applications
05

ROC Registration & Company Filings

Incorporation of companies and LLPs, and every annual and event-based filing with the MCA.

  • Company / LLP incorporation
  • AOC-4, MGT-7, ADT-1, DPT-3
  • DIR-3 KYC, DIN & charge filings
06

Udyam & MSME Registration

Udyam registration and the allied registrations a small business needs to start and stay compliant.

  • Udyam / MSME certificate
  • Shop & establishment, IEC, FSSAI
  • PAN, TAN & professional tax
07

Books Maintenance in Tally

Day-to-day accounting maintained in Tally, reconciled monthly and closed ready for audit.

  • Voucher entry & ledger scrutiny
  • Bank & party reconciliation
  • Monthly MIS, P&L and balance sheet
08

TDS & Payroll Compliance

Quarterly TDS returns, correction statements and certificate issuance.

  • 24Q / 26Q / 27Q / 27EQ
  • Form 16 & 16A, TRACES defaults
  • PF, ESI & professional tax
09

Audit & Assurance

Statutory audit, tax audit under Sec 44AB, internal and stock audit assignments.

  • Form 3CA / 3CB-3CD
  • Trust audit — Form 10B / 10BB
  • Internal & stock audit
10

Bank Concurrent Audit

Empanelled concurrent auditors for Indian Bank, Canara Bank and APGB branches.

  • Monthly concurrent audit reports
  • Revenue leakage verification
  • KYC & advances review
11

Project Reports & Loan Documentation

Bank loan project reports across dairy, poultry, manufacturing and MSME sectors.

  • DSCR & cash flow projections
  • CMA data preparation
  • Subsidy scheme documentation
12

Trust, Partnership & Land Documentation

Trust registration support, partnership deeds, Devasthanam matters and land alienation petitions.

Questions

FAQ

What services does the firm provide?

Income tax and GST return filing, scrutiny and assessment cases, appeal matters before CIT(A) and NFAC, ROC registration and company filings, Udyam and MSME registration, maintenance of books of account in Tally, TDS and payroll compliance, audit and assurance, bank concurrent audit, project reports for bank loans, and trust, partnership and land documentation.

Do you maintain books of account in Tally?

Yes — we maintain day-to-day books in Tally, carry out bank and party reconciliation every month, and hand over a closed set of accounts ready for audit, along with a monthly MIS, profit & loss account and balance sheet.

Can you handle a scrutiny notice or an appeal?

Yes. We handle scrutiny under Sec 143(2), reassessment under Sec 148 and 148A, and penalty proceedings, and we draft the grounds of appeal and statement of facts and represent clients before CIT(A) and NFAC.

Can you register a company, LLP or Udyam for me?

Yes — company and LLP incorporation with the MCA, all annual and event-based ROC filings, and Udyam / MSME registration along with allied registrations such as PAN, TAN, IEC and professional tax.

Do you help with GST show-cause notices?

Yes — from analysing the notice to drafting and filing the reply, including DRC-01 and DRC-03 matters.

Do you prepare project reports for bank loans?

Yes — for MSME, agriculture, dairy, poultry and manufacturing units, including DSCR workings and cash flow projections, for submission to the financing bank.

How can I send you documents?

Through the secure Client Portal linked above, or in person at our Kadapa office. The portal shows a checklist of exactly which documents each service needs.

How do I know who is working on my matter?

Every job in the portal shows the assigned team member, the reviewer, the current stage, and the target completion date. You are notified whenever the status changes.
